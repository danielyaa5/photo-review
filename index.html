Compress videos

Each video is downloaded from iCloud at full quality, re-encoded on this Mac, and added to Photos as a new item with the same date, location, favorite and albums. The original stays until you delete it, unless you picked Compress & delete for it or tick the box below.

Leave room on your Mac: downloaded originals can grow the Photos library. Use Optimize Mac Storage in Photos' iCloud settings and smaller batches if space is tight. After checking the smaller copies and their iCloud upload, you can permanently remove only their replaced originals from Recently Deleted. This deletes those originals across your iCloud Photos devices and ends recovery. Photos storage guide

macOS asks you to confirm once, when the whole queue has finished, not after each video. Deleted originals stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days.

HDR videos stay HDR, and HDR, ProRes and other 10-bit videos are always saved as HEVC so they keep their full colour depth. Edits are baked in, and captions, keywords and people tags don't carry over.
